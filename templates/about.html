CustomerIQ – E-Commerce Customer Churn Prediction System

CustomerIQ is a Flask + Scikit-learn customer intelligence platform that loads a pre-trained Random Forest pipeline and an operating threshold to predict per-customer churn probability, classify customers into High Risk / Low Risk tiers, and visualize churn drivers across the e-commerce customer base.

Dataset

Approximately 50,000 customer records with 26 columns. A synthetic CustomerID (format CUST00001) was generated for identification — these are not real customer identities.

Model

  • Random Forest Classifier (200 estimators, max_depth=15, class_weight='balanced')
  • Pipeline: median imputation (numeric), most-frequent imputation (categorical), OneHotEncoder
  • Trained on 80/20 stratified split
  • Classification threshold: 0.43
Known limitation (data leakage). The current cleaned dataset was imputed before the train/test split. This may introduce information leakage and inflate the reported metrics. The model should eventually be retrained with preprocessing fit only on the training split before its metrics are treated as final.

Important Notes

  • Metrics shown are from the original notebook, not independently re-validated.
  • Segments are rule-based, not ML clustering.
  • Feature importance ≠ causation.
  • The Churned target column is never displayed on prediction result pages.
